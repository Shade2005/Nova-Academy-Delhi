Academics & Batches

Which classes and subjects do you offer tuition for? expand_more
We offer coaching for students from Nursery to Class 10, covering all core subjects: Mathematics, Science, English, Hindi, and Social Studies. Our curriculum is mapped to both CBSE and ICSE board syllabi. Each subject is taught with a focus on building a strong conceptual foundation, not just exam preparation.
What is the batch size and student-to-teacher ratio? expand_more
We strictly cap every batch at 15 students per teacher, maintaining a 1:15 student-to-faculty ratio. This is one of the most important differences between Nova Academy and typical coaching centers in Okhla or Jamia Nagar, where batches of 40–50 students are common. Our small batches mean every student gets direct attention and personal guidance — no student is left behind.
Is the coaching aligned with CBSE and ICSE boards? expand_more
Yes. Our school coaching is fully aligned with the latest CBSE and ICSE guidelines. Our faculty members are experienced in preparing students for both school examinations and future competitive exams. We track each student's progress systematically so that no gap in understanding goes unaddressed.
What are the batch timings? expand_more
We run multiple batch slots to fit around school timings and work schedules. Batches are available in the morning, afternoon, and evening, on both weekdays and weekends. WhatsApp us at +91 89292 43913 to check the current available slots for your preferred class and subject.

Upskilling Courses

Do I need prior experience for Web Design, Digital Marketing, or Graphic Design courses? expand_more
No prior experience is required for any of our upskilling programs. All four courses — Digital Marketing, Web Design, Web Development, and Graphic Design — are designed to take complete beginners step-by-step to a job-ready, professional standard. By the end of the course, you will have a portfolio of real projects to show employers or clients.
Who are the upskilling courses designed for? expand_more
Our upskilling courses are ideal for college students who want practical, employable skills; working professionals looking to switch careers or add a digital skill set; and small business owners or entrepreneurs who want to market their own businesses online. We serve students from across South Delhi, including Okhla, Batla House, Shaheen Bagh, Abul Fazal Enclave, and Jamia Nagar.
